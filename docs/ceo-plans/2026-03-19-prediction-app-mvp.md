## Vision

### 10x Check
Transform a single-use polling tool into a sticky social accountability network where users build a "Global Reputation Score" based on their predictive accuracy and goal-completion rates. Enhance the experience with gamified interactions (tear-off animations) and decentralized trust (AI objective verifiers).

- UI/UX: Interactive "stamp" to vote and "tear-off" contract animation upon resolution.

- Trust/Verification: AI Vision model integration. Creators must upload an image as proof, which AI verifies against the original goal description.
